## 9. AI ratings — what they are and are not

Our software produces attribute scores and an overall rating from your videos.

**These are estimates, and they are often wrong.**

- A rating depends heavily on the quality of the footage. Distance, lighting,
  camera movement, a crowded frame or a short clip can all produce a low or
  unreliable score for a player who is genuinely good.
- Where our analysis is not confident, **the app marks the number as such**.
  Pay attention to those markers — they are not decoration.
- A rating is **not** a professional assessment of ability, and a low rating
  does not mean you are a poor player.
- Ratings change as we improve the analysis. Yours may go down for reasons
  that have nothing to do with you.

**We do not promise** that any scout will view your profile, contact you,
invite you to a trial, or offer you anything. No opportunity of any kind is
guaranteed by using Matobev, at any rating.

**Scouts:** treat ratings as one signal among many. Confirm with real footage
and, where possible, in person. Nothing on this platform replaces watching a
player play.
