crowded frame can all produce a low or unreliable score for a player who is
genuinely good. Where the analysis is not confident, the app marks the number
as such — pay attention to those markers.

- A rating is not a professional assessment of ability.
- A low rating does not mean you are a poor player.
- We do not promise that any scout will contact you, that you will be invited
  to a trial, or that any opportunity will result from using Matobev.

Scouts should treat ratings as one signal among many and confirm with real
footage and, where possible, in person. Nothing on this platform replaces
watching a player play.
