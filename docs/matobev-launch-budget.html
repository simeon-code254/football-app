The cheapest real decision in this budget

Launching Android first saves KES 13,266 in year one, and it is the right call on the evidence: your users are on Infinix, itel and Tecno devices running Android Go. iOS can follow once there is revenue to justify the annual fee. That single choice is a fifth of the minimum launch cost.

E · What happens as it grows — the number to watch

Storage is cheap. Egress is not. Egress is bandwidth consumed when someone watches a video, so it scales with how much the app is used, not with how many people sign up — and every player is free forever.

Assumes 40 MB per compressed highlight and two videos per player. Supabase Pro includes 100 GB storage and 250 GB egress; overages are $0.021/GB for storage and $0.09/GB for egress.

This is the budget's real risk

At 10,000 players the bill is roughly KES 96,000 a month, and KES 91,000 of that is people watching videos. Storage is only about KES 1,900 of it. Success on the player side raises the bill with no revenue attached, because players never pay.

Three mitigations, in order of effect: compress harder — 720p at a lower bitrate can halve 40 MB and halves the entire egress column; put a CDN in front of video so repeat views are cached rather than re-served; and price scout subscriptions to cover egress, since scouts are the ones doing most of the watching.

F · What this budget does not include

Not includedWhy, and roughly what it would cost
Your timeThe largest real cost by far, and the one a spreadsheet always omits.
Moderation and verificationThe trust positioning depends on a human checking scout documents and handling reports. It cannot be automated away. At any real scale this becomes the biggest line in the budget.
Hosted AI computeAnalysis runs on your machine today. A small always-on CPU instance is roughly $20–40/mo (KES 2,700–5,400); per-upload serverless may be cheaper at low volume.
Marketing and seedingZero here. But a scouting app with thin inventory is not worth opening twice, so getting the first players and scouts onto the platform is the spend that decides whether the rest matters.
Apple hardwareNot needed — EAS builds iOS in the cloud. You only need a Mac if you want a local iOS simulator.
Trademark registrationKIPI trademark filing is roughly KES 5,000–10,000 per class. Worth doing before anyone else registers "Matobev".
VAT and taxBelow the VAT registration threshold there is nothing to remit, but income is still taxable. Speak to an accountant when revenue starts.

G · Recommendation

What I would actually spend

Launch Android only, incorporated, legally reviewed. That is KES 103,268 for the first year, or KES 118,758 with a 15% contingency — and it defers KES 13,266 of Apple fees until there is a reason to pay them.

Of that, KES 4,000 is genuinely non-negotiable (ODPC registration, because you process biometric data) and roughly KES 50,650 is the legal work — incorporation plus advocate review. That legal spend is not overhead. On a platform holding minors' biometric data, run by a sole proprietor with no corporate veil, it is the cheapest insurance available.
