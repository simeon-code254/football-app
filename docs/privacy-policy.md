# Matobev Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 20 August 2026**

> **⚠️ NEEDS LEGAL REVIEW AND TWO FACTUAL COMPLETIONS BEFORE PUBLISHING.**
> Everything below describes what the Matobev app and database actually do,
> checked against the schema, the Edge Functions and the live project. It is
> accurate, but it is not a lawyer's work. Two things only you can supply are
> marked `[[...]]` — the registered legal entity and its address. A policy
> naming no accountable entity is not compliant no matter how accurate the
> rest of it is.

Matobev connects footballers in Africa with scouts. This policy explains what
we collect, why, who sees it, and how to get rid of it.

We have written it plainly on purpose. Many of our users are teenagers, and a
policy a 15-year-old cannot read is not really a policy.

## 2. What we collect

**When you create an account**
- Your email address and password (handled by our authentication provider; we
  never see your password)
- Your name
- Your phone number, if you provide one
- Whether you are a player or a scout — this cannot be changed later

**If you are a player**
- Date of birth, gender, nationality
- Playing position(s), preferred foot, whether you are a goalkeeper
- Height, weight, current club, jersey number, years playing
- A short bio, and any Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or Facebook links you add
- Your profile photo

**If you are a scout**
- Your organisation, a bio, your country
- Documents you upload to verify you are a real scout

**Videos you upload**
- The video file and a thumbnail
- Title, description, match name, opponent and tags you enter
- If you request AI analysis, the point in the video where you tagged yourself

**What our analysis produces**
- Attribute scores (pace, physical, positioning and others) and an overall
  rating, plus how confident the analysis was in each one

**How you use the app**
- Messages you send, and any attachments
- Which profiles you view, and which profiles viewed yours
- Likes, saves, comments, follows and endorsements
- Trials you apply to

**Technical data**
- A notification token for your device, if you enable notifications
- Crash reports and error diagnostics

## 3. AI analysis of your videos — read this part

If you upload a video and ask for AI analysis, our system **measures your body
as it moves through the video**. It estimates the position of your joints
frame by frame to work out things like how fast you accelerate.

This is processing of your physical characteristics. Under laws including the
United States' COPPA rules (as amended, in force 22 April 2026) and the EU's
GDPR, that is a special category of information when it concerns a child, and
it needs its own separate permission rather than being bundled into "using the
app".

So:

- **If you are 18 or over**, requesting AI analysis is your decision.
- **If you are under 18**, a parent or guardian must confirm it first. The app
  will not let you upload a video for analysis until they have. You can still
  post highlight videos without analysis — those are never measured this way.
- **We do not use your videos or your body measurements to train AI models.**
  They are used to produce your own ratings, and nothing else.

Your ratings are estimates produced by software. They are frequently
uncertain, and the app tells you when they are. They are not a professional
assessment of your ability.

## 4. If you are under 18

You must be at least **13** to have an account at all.

If you are between 13 and 17:
- You can create an account and post highlight videos.
- A parent or guardian must confirm consent before your videos can be
  analysed. You send them a link from inside the app; they open it and decide.
- We record only their name, email, and what they agreed to — the minimum
  needed to evidence that consent was actually given.
- They can withdraw consent at any time by contacting **support@matobev.com**.
- Your profile is **not** visible to people who are not signed in. Adults'
  profiles can be browsed by anyone; yours cannot.

Confirmation by link is a reasonable method, but it is not the strongest
method that exists. If you believe an account has been created without a
guardian's knowledge, email us and we will remove it.

---

## 5. Who can see your information

**Scouts using Matobev** can see player profiles, videos, ratings and the
information you put on your profile. That is what the app is for.

**Other players** can see your public profile and videos.

**People who are not signed in** can browse profiles of players aged 18 and
over. Profiles of users under 18 are hidden from them entirely.

**Nobody** can see your email address, your phone number, your private
messages, or the documents a scout uploads for verification.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it for advertising.

## 7. Why we are allowed to use your data

- To provide the service you asked for — your account, profile, videos and
  messages.
- With your consent — AI analysis of your videos, and push notifications. You
  can withdraw either at any time.
- Our legitimate interest in keeping the platform safe — moderation, blocking
  abusive accounts, investigating reports.
- To meet legal obligations where they apply.

---

## 8. How long we keep it

- While your account is open, we keep your profile, videos and messages.
- **When you delete your account, we delete it.** Your videos and files are
  removed from storage and your database records are deleted. This is
  immediate and it is not reversible.
- Records that consent was given are kept after deletion only where we are
  legally required to evidence it.
- Suspension and moderation records are kept so a banned account cannot simply
  be recreated.

---

## 9. Your rights

You can:

- **See** what we hold about you — most of it is visible in the app.
- **Correct** it — edit your profile at any time.
- **Delete** it — Settings → Delete account. No email required, no waiting.
- **Withdraw consent** — turn off notifications in settings; contact us to
  withdraw guardian consent for analysis.
- **Object or complain** — email us, or complain to your national data
  protection authority. In Kenya that is the Office of the Data Protection
  Commissioner.

---

## 10. Security

Access to data is enforced in the database itself, not only in the app, so
your private information stays private even if someone calls our API directly.
Videos are stored privately and served through short-lived signed links.
Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and never reach us.

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a breach affecting you, we will
tell you and the relevant regulator.

---

## 11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app
before it takes effect. The date at the top always reflects the current
version.
